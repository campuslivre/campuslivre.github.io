wordcloudDraws words sized by their frequency or by a weight.

contourcComputes contour line coordinates without drawing them.
contourfDraws filled contour plots.
contoursliceDraws contour lines on slice planes of 3-D volume data.

ezcontourfFilled contour plot of a function of two variables given as a handle or expression.
fcontourContour plot of a function of two variables, given as a handle.

sliceDraws slice planes through volumetric data.
smooth3Smooths a 3-D data array with a box or Gaussian filter.
sphereGenerates the coordinates of a unit sphere, or draws it.
