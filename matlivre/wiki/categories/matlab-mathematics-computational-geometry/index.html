Elementary Polygons

inpolygonTests which points lie inside or on the edge of a polygon.

nearestvertexIndex of the polyshape vertex closest to each query point.
nsidedpolyCreates a regular polygon with a given number of sides, as a polyshape.
numboundariesNumber of boundaries of a polyshape, counting outer boundaries and holes.
numsidesTotal number of sides (vertices) of a polyshape.
overlapsTests whether two polyshapes share any area.
polyareaArea of a polygon given by its vertex coordinates.

rectintArea of intersection between axis-aligned rectangles.
regionsSplits a polyshape into its disconnected regions.

rmsliversRemoves thin parts of a polyshape narrower than a given width.

sortboundariesReorders the boundaries of a polyshape by a chosen criterion.

cartesianToBarycentricConverts Cartesian points to barycentric coordinates relative to given triangles or tetrahedra.
cartToBaryLegacy form of cartesianToBarycentric for TriRep and DelaunayTri objects.

circumcentersLegacy form of circumcenter for TriRep and DelaunayTri objects.
edgeAttachmentsTriangles or tetrahedra attached to given edges of a triangulation.
edgesAll edges of a triangulation, as pairs of vertex indices.
faceNormalUnit normal vector of each triangle of a triangulated surface.
faceNormalsLegacy form of faceNormal for TriRep and DelaunayTri objects.
featureEdgesSharp edges of a triangulated surface, selected by the angle between adjacent faces.
freeBoundaryBoundary facets of a triangulation: edges of a 2-D mesh or faces of a 3-D one that touch only one simplex.
incenterIncenters and inscribed-circle radii of the triangles or tetrahedra of a triangulation.
incentersLegacy form of incenter for TriRep and DelaunayTri objects.

trimeshPlots a triangular mesh as wireframe edges.
