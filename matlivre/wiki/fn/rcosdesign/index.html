rcosdesign

Designs a raised-cosine FIR filter, normal or square-root, for pulse shaping in digital communications.

Category: FIR design

Syntax

h = rcosdesign(beta,span,sps)

Square-root raised-cosine filter (the default).

h = rcosdesign(beta,span,sps,shape)

Chooses 'sqrt' or 'normal'.

Description

Two square-root raised-cosine filters in a row (one at the transmitter, one at the receiver) give a raised-cosine response with no intersymbol interference at the symbol instants. That is why the square-root shape is the default.

The filter is truncated to span symbols, so a longer span gives a response closer to the ideal one.

Input arguments

beta
Roll-off factor, between 0 and 1. Larger values use more bandwidth and give faster decaying tails.
span
Number of symbol periods the filter covers.
sps
Samples per symbol.
shape
'sqrt' (default) or 'normal'.

Output arguments

h
Row vector of span*sps+1 symmetric coefficients. For 'sqrt' the energy sum(h.^2) is 1.

Example

Square-root raised cosine with roll-off 0.25

h = rcosdesign(0.25,4,3);
disp(length(h))
disp(round(h*10000)/10000)
fprintf('%.4f\n', sum(h.^2))
