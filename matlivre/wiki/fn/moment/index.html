moment next release

Central moment of a given order of a sample: the mean of the deviations from the mean raised to that power.

Category: Descriptive Statistics and Visualization > Descriptive Statistics

Syntax

m = moment(x, order)

Central moment of the given order, for a vector or for each column of a matrix.

m = moment(x, order, dim)

Along dimension dim.

m = moment(x, order, 'omitnan')

Ignores NaN values.

Description

moment(x, k) computes mean((x - mean(x)).^k), dividing by the number of observations. The second moment is the population variance, and the third and fourth moments are the building blocks of skewness and kurtosis.

Input arguments

x
Numeric array of observations.
order
Order of the moment, a scalar. Order 1 gives 0 and order 2 is the variance with divisor n.
dim
Dimension to operate along. Default: first non-singleton dimension.

Output arguments

m
Moment, with the size of x reduced to 1 along the chosen dimension.

Examples

Second and third moments

y = [1 2 3 4 10];
disp(moment(y, 2))
disp(moment(y, 3))
disp(var(y, 1))
Output
    10
    36
    10

Skewness from moments

y = [1 2 3 4 10];
s = moment(y, 3) / moment(y, 2)^1.5;
fprintf('%.4f %.4f\n', s, skewness(y))
Output
1.1384 1.1384

Notes

The text options 'central' and 'absolute' are not accepted and give an error. Only the central moment is computed.
