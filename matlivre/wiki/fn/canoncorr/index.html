canoncorr next release

Canonical correlation analysis: finds linear combinations of two sets of variables that are as correlated as possible.

Category: ANOVA > Analysis of Variance and Covariance

Syntax

[A, B] = canoncorr(X, Y)

Returns the coefficients of the canonical variables of X and of Y.

[A, B, r] = canoncorr(X, Y)

Also returns the canonical correlations.

[A, B, r, U, V, stats] = canoncorr(X, Y)

Also returns the canonical scores and the test statistics for each correlation.

Description

canoncorr looks for the pair of combinations, one of the columns of X and one of the columns of Y, whose correlation is highest. The next pair is the most correlated one that is uncorrelated with the first, and so on. The scores U(:,k) and V(:,k) of the same pair have correlation r(k).

In stats, the k-th entry of p tests the hypothesis that the k-th and all later canonical correlations are zero. Rows with NaN in either matrix are left out.

Input arguments

X
n-by-p matrix: n observations of p variables.
Y
n-by-q matrix: the same n observations of q other variables.

Output arguments

A
p-by-d coefficients that combine the columns of X, where d is the smaller rank of the two sets.
B
q-by-d coefficients that combine the columns of Y.
r
1-by-d canonical correlations, from the largest to the smallest.
U
n-by-d canonical scores of X (centered X times A).
V
n-by-d canonical scores of Y (centered Y times B).
stats
Structure with the Wilks lambda test of each correlation: Wilks, df1, df2, F, pF, chisq, pChisq, dfe and p.

Example

Canonical correlations of two sets

t = (1:12)';
X = [sin(t) cos(2*t) t/12];
Y = [X(:,1)+0.3*cos(3*t) X(:,2).^2+0.1*t];
[A, B, r, U, V, st] = canoncorr(X, Y);
disp(round(r*10000)/10000)
disp(size(A))
disp(round(st.p*10000)/10000)
fprintf('%.4f\n', corr(U(:,1), V(:,1)))
Output
    0.9661    0.7108
     3     2
    0.0001    0.0585
0.9661

Notes

The signs of the canonical vectors (columns of A, B, U and V) can be the opposite of MATLAB's, because MatLivre's SVD does not follow the LAPACK sign convention. The correlations r and the statistics in stats are not affected.
