residuez

Converts between a ratio of polynomials in z^-1 and its partial-fraction form, with residues, poles and direct terms.

Category: Filtering

Syntax

[r, p, k] = residuez(b, a)

Partial-fraction expansion of b(z)/a(z).

[b, a] = residuez(r, p, k)

Rebuilds the polynomial coefficients from residues, poles and direct terms.

Description

The result has the form b(z)/a(z) = r(1)/(1 - p(1) z^-1) + ... + r(n)/(1 - p(n) z^-1) + k(1) + k(2) z^-1 + .... Each pole p(i) contributes a term r(i) * p(i)^n to the impulse response, so the expansion shows directly how the response of the filter is made.

This is the same idea as residue, but for polynomials in z^-1, which is the usual form of digital filters. Called with three inputs, it goes the other way.

Input arguments

b
Numerator coefficients, in increasing powers of z^-1.
a
Denominator coefficients, in increasing powers of z^-1. The first one must not be zero.
r
Residues, a vector.
p
Poles, a vector with the same length as r.
k
Direct terms, empty when the numerator is shorter than the denominator.

Output arguments

r
Residues, a column vector.
p
Poles, a column vector.
k
Direct terms, the polynomial part, empty when length(b) < length(a).
b, a
Rebuilt numerator and denominator coefficients.

Examples

Expand and rebuild

b = [1 2];
a = [1 -0.5 -0.06];
[r, p, k] = residuez(b, a);
disp(round(r.' * 1e4) / 1e4)
disp(round(p.' * 1e4) / 1e4)
disp(isempty(k))
[b2, a2] = residuez(r, p, k);
disp(round(b2 * 1e4) / 1e4)
disp(round(a2 * 1e4) / 1e4)
Output
    3.7143   -2.7143
    0.6000   -0.1000
   1
     1     2
    1.0000   -0.5000   -0.0600

With a direct term

[r, p, k] = residuez([1 0 0 1], [1 -0.5]);
disp(r.')
disp(p.')
disp(k)
Output
     9
    0.5000
    -8    -4    -2

Notes

Poles of equal size (a real one and a complex pair) are ordered by rounding noise in the eigenvalues, so their order can differ from MATLAB. For a triple pole, MATLAB finds it only to about 1e-5, while MatLivre refines it to the exact value.
