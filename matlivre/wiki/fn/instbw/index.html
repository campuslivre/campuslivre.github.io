instbw

Estimates the instantaneous bandwidth of a signal over time.

Category: Spectral measurements

Syntax

bw = instbw(x, fs)

Instantaneous bandwidth of x, sampled at fs Hz.

[bw, t] = instbw(x, fs)

Also returns the time of each estimate.

bw = instbw(x, ___, 'FrequencyLimits', [f1 f2])

Restricts the calculation to a frequency band.

bw = instbw(p, f, t)

Uses a power spectrogram p with frequency vector f and time vector t.

Description

instbw computes a spectrogram of the signal and, at every time slice, measures how widely the power is spread around its mean frequency. The result is that spread (a standard deviation) times ScaleFactor. A single clean tone gives a small value, and a slice with several components gives a larger one.

The estimates come at the time points of the spectrogram, so there are far fewer than samples in x. Use instfreq to get the center frequency that this bandwidth is measured around.

Input arguments

x
Signal, real or complex. Columns are channels.
fs
Sampling rate in Hz. A time vector can be given instead.
'FrequencyLimits'
Two-element vector [f1 f2] in Hz; f2 cannot exceed fs/2.
'ScaleFactor'
Positive number that scales the spread. The default is sqrt(4*pi).

Output arguments

bw
Instantaneous bandwidth in Hz, one row per time estimate and one column per channel.
t
Time of each estimate, a column vector.

Example

Bandwidth grows when a second tone appears

fs = 1000; t = (0:255)'/fs;
y = exp(1i*2*pi*100*t) + (t > 0.128).*exp(1i*2*pi*300*t);
[bw, tm] = instbw(y, fs);
fprintf('%d estimates, first at %.3f s\n', numel(bw), tm(1));
disp(round(bw([1 5 10 15 20 25 29])'))
Output
29 estimates, first at 0.016 s
   116   116   116   380   374   374   372
