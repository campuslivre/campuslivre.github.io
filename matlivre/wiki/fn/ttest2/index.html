ttest2 next release

Two-sample t-test: tests whether two independent samples have equal means, and returns the decision, p-value and confidence interval for the difference.

Category: Probability Distributions and Hypothesis Tests > Univariate Continuous Distributions

Syntax

h = ttest2(x, y)

Tests whether x and y have equal means, assuming equal variances.

h = ttest2(x, y, 'Vartype', 'unequal')

Does not assume equal variances (Satterthwaite degrees of freedom).

[h, p, ci, stats] = ttest2(___, Name, Value)

Also returns the p-value, confidence interval and statistics. Options 'Alpha', 'Tail', 'Vartype' and 'Dim'.

Description

Use ttest2 when the two samples are independent, for example two groups of different subjects. For measurements on the same subjects before and after, use the paired form of ttest.

With 'Vartype','equal' the two variances are pooled and the degrees of freedom are n1 + n2 - 2. With 'unequal' the degrees of freedom are not an integer in general, and the test is more careful when the group variances differ.

Input arguments

x, y
Two samples. They may have different lengths. For matrices, columns are compared one by one.
'Alpha'
Significance level. Default 0.05.
'Tail'
'both' (default), 'right' or 'left': the alternative is that the mean of x differs from, is greater than, or is less than the mean of y.
'Vartype'
'equal' (default) or 'unequal'.
'Dim'
Dimension to test along.

Output arguments

h
1 if the null hypothesis of equal means is rejected, otherwise 0.
p
p-value of the test.
ci
Confidence interval for the difference of means mean(x) - mean(y).
stats
Structure with fields tstat, df and sd.

Examples

Equal variances

a = [10 12 9 11 13 10];
b = [14 15 13 16 12 15 14];
[h, p, ci, st] = ttest2(a, b);
fprintf('h=%d p=%.4f\n', h, p)
disp(round(ci*10000)/10000)
disp(round([st.tstat st.df]*10000)/10000)
Output
h=1 p=0.0014
   -5.0290   -1.5900
   -4.2362   11.0000

Unequal variances

a = [10 12 9 11 13 10];
b = [14 15 13 16 12 15 14];
[h, p, ci, st] = ttest2(a, b, 'Vartype', 'unequal');
fprintf('h=%d p=%.4f\n', h, p)
disp(round([st.tstat st.df]*10000)/10000)
Output
h=1 p=0.0017
   -4.2044   10.3156
