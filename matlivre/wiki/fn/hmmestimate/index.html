hmmestimate next release

Estimates the transition and emission matrices of a hidden Markov model from a sequence of symbols and the known sequence of states.

Category: Cluster Analysis and Anomaly Detection > Hidden Markov Models

Syntax

[TRANS, EMIS] = hmmestimate(seq, states)

Maximum likelihood estimates from the counts in the data.

[TRANS, EMIS] = hmmestimate(seq, states, 'Pseudotransitions', PT, 'Pseudoemissions', PE)

Adds pseudocounts, which avoids zero probabilities for events that were not observed.

[TRANS, EMIS] = hmmestimate(seq, states, 'Symbols', symbols)

Maps numeric symbol values to the columns of EMIS.

Description

When you know the true states, as in a training set, the best estimate of each probability is a ratio of counts. Rows of a state that never occurred (or never had a successor) come out as zeros, because there is nothing to estimate. Pseudocounts fill them with a small prior belief.

When the states are hidden, this function does not apply: the model parameters have to be fitted by an iterative method such as the Baum-Welch algorithm.

Input arguments

seq
Observed symbols: a vector of positive integers.
states
True state at each step: a vector of positive integers with the same length as seq.
'Pseudotransitions'
m-by-m matrix of counts added to the transition counts.
'Pseudoemissions'
m-by-n matrix of counts added to the emission counts. It also sets the number of symbols.
'Symbols'
Numeric vector with the n symbol values. Sets the number of columns of EMIS.

Output arguments

TRANS
m-by-m transition matrix: the fraction of times that state i was followed by state j.
EMIS
m-by-n emission matrix: the fraction of times that state i produced symbol k.

Examples

Estimate from known states

seq    = [1 2 6 6 6 3 4 6 6 6 2 6 6 6 1 6 5 6 6 6];
states = [1 1 2 2 2 1 1 2 2 2 1 2 2 2 1 2 2 2 2 2];
[TR, EM] = hmmestimate(seq, states);
disp(round(TR*10000)/10000)
disp(round(EM*10000)/10000)
Output
    0.3333    0.6667
    0.2308    0.7692
    0.3333    0.3333    0.1667    0.1667         0         0
         0         0         0         0    0.0714    0.9286

With pseudocounts

seq    = [1 2 6 6 6 3 4 6 6 6 2 6 6 6 1 6 5 6 6 6];
states = [1 1 2 2 2 1 1 2 2 2 1 2 2 2 1 2 2 2 2 2];
[TR, EM] = hmmestimate(seq, states, 'Pseudotransitions', ones(2), 'Pseudoemissions', ones(2,6));
disp(round(TR*10000)/10000)
disp(all(EM(:) > 0))
Output
    0.3750    0.6250
    0.2667    0.7333
   1

Notes

seq must be numeric: a cell array of text symbols is rejected. The option 'Statenames' is not supported and raises an error.
